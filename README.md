## Project Description:
A Salesforce-based CRM application to manage customer relationships for TheChaiShop&Co. The system automates sales calculations, customer records, reports, dashboards and birthday email notifications.

## Key Features:
1.  Customer, Employer and Enterprise Objects
2.  Custom Tabs and Lightning App
3.  Validation Rules
4.  Email Templates and Flows
5.  Reports and Dashboards
6.  Order Quantity and Total Bill Auto Calculation

## Technologies Used:
- Salesforce Developer Org
- Apex, Flow Builder, Process Builder
- Reports & Dashboards
